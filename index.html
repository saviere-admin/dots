FAQ

Questions people ask before switching.

What is the core difference between dots. and conventional toothpaste?+

dots. is a solid-state oral care tablet designed around Nano-Hydroxyapatite and a waterless format. It removes the dead-weight water and discarded tube architecture common to traditional pastes.

Does it help with remineralization?+

Yes. The formula is designed around Nano-Hydroxyapatite, a mineral system commonly associated with enamel support and biomimetic oral care.

Why is this better for travel?+

The format is compact, spill-proof, and lighter than liquid toothpaste, making it ideal for travel and personal carry with fewer compliance concerns.

How does it reduce waste?+

By removing heavy water content, conventional plastic tubes, and composite lamination from the product and shipping equation, the overall environmental footprint is materially lower.
